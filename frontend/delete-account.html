Delete your account

You can delete a Nexora customer or driver account here, including after uninstalling the app.

What happens

Deletion removes your sign-in, profile, saved addresses, notifications, location history, and personal details from completed journeys. It revokes your sessions. This cannot be undone.

Limited transaction records (amounts, dates and payment references) and security or dispute records are retained for reconciliation and fraud prevention. Payment providers may retain their own required financial records.

Complete or cancel active journeys first. Pending card payments and driver settlements must be resolved. If you cannot sign in or complete deletion, email tanzeelsam@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject “Nexora account deletion”. Never send a password or PIN. Support will verify account ownership before acting.
