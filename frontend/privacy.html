A plain-language description of the current Nexora service. Updated 5 November 2026.

About this notice

Nexora is preparing for public launch. This page describes the current application’s data handling. The operator’s legal identity and detailed transaction/security retention schedule still need to be published before public bookings open.

Business details will be published before public bookings open.

Privacy and support: tanzeelsam@gmail.com.

Information the service handles

  • Accounts: names, email addresses, phone numbers, password hashes and session records. Recovery codes are stored as hashes.
  • Phone sign-in: when you sign in with your phone, your number is sent to our text message provider to deliver a one-time code. We keep the number, a keyed hash of the code, the request time and your network address to prevent abuse. Codes expire after five minutes.
  • Journeys: pickup and destination addresses and coordinates, route details, fares, status updates, ratings and payment records.
  • Deliveries: package details, sender and recipient contact information, handoff PINs, recipient name, signature and delivery timestamps.
  • Drivers: vehicle and onboarding details, availability, earnings and location updates while using the driver service.
  • Support: request messages, journey references and replies visible to authorized support staff.
  • Optional notifications: a browser push subscription if you enable alerts.

How information is used and shared

These details support account access, matching and dispatch, route estimates, journey tracking, handoff verification and payment records. Assigned drivers receive the job information they need. Authorized operations staff can access records for service administration.

Configured providers process data for hosting, database storage, address search and routing, notifications and card payments. The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles and address search uses Mapbox when enabled. Loading maps or externally hosted fonts and libraries sends ordinary network request information, including your IP address, to those providers. When card checkout is enabled, Stripe handles card entry and payment processing.

A delivery tracking link provides access to delivery progress to anyone who has the link. Share it only with people who need to follow the delivery.

On your device

Nexora uses session storage for sign-in tokens, recovery from interrupted booking requests and planning forms. Route and package drafts expire after 30 minutes. Recent journey details can be saved for up to two hours to help during connection loss; the app marks them as saved, not live. These may include addresses, contact details and handoff PINs for your journey. Signing out removes these snapshots and drafts. Public page layouts and scripts are cached separately, without API responses or map tiles. Addresses are not placed in navigation URLs.

Location access is requested when you choose a location action. You can refuse or revoke access in your browser or Android app settings. On Android, choosing Go online starts a driver shift service with an ongoing notification. It sends fresh location updates and checks for assigned jobs while another app is open or the screen is locked. It stops when you go offline, sign out, close the app from recent apps, or Android stops it. It does not start at device boot. Job alerts require notification permission and internet access. In a browser, keep the driver page open; web alerts depend on browser support.

Access, correction and deletion

Use Delete account to verify your password and remove a customer or driver account. You can also request deletion by emailing tanzeelsam@gmail.com, including if you no longer have the app. We will verify ownership before acting. You do not need to create another account to request deletion.

Self-service deletion revokes sessions, removes profile and contact details, device notification subscriptions and location history, and redacts your booking addresses, handoff details and support messages. Active journeys and unresolved payments or settlements must be resolved first; contact support if you cannot do this in the app.

Limited transaction records (amounts, dates, payment references and non-public account identifiers) and necessary safety/security records may be retained for financial reconciliation, dispute handling and legal obligations. Backups and short-lived request recovery records are not immediately overwritten. The operator must publish an approved retention period before paid public bookings open. Email support for access, correction, export or a deletion request that needs assistance.

Area interest and merchant deliveries

If you register interest in an area, we store your locality, email, chosen service, and consent time to plan coverage and contact you about availability. Ask support to remove your entry. The merchant desk stores your business name, optional saved pickup, order references, and goods-payment records. Drivers see the amount they must collect; authorized operations staff record cash receipt and merchant settlement. These records are separate from delivery fees. Deleting your account removes your business profile and saved pickup. Outstanding goods-payment balances must first be resolved; limited transaction records remain under the retention terms above.
